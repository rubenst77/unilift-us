Installation methods

How do you install your elevators?

We configure the lifting equipment and accessories around your shaft, load and workflow. Common lifting configurations include 1:1 and 2:1. The complete setup depends on the shaft, load and installation method.

Common configuration

1:1 lifting configuration

The hoist travels with the platform. One part of rope supports the load.

Common configuration

2:1 lifting configuration

The hoist stays fixed. The rope runs through a pulley on the platform, so two parts of rope support the load.

In the shaft

How it works in the shaft

During elevator installation the traction hoist lifts personnel and equipment in the shaft. The wire rope passes through the hoist instead of winding onto a drum, so lifting height follows the length of rope deployed.

Why a traction hoist

No drum

Height follows the rope

Certifications

Certifications and US scope

North American UNILIFT models are certified to UL 1323 and CSA Z271 for powered suspended scaffold and suspended access applications. FAS-Tech also configures these traction hoists for elevator installation.
