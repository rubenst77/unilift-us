Installation configuration

How do you install your elevators?

Start with your current method. We configure the lifting equipment and accessories around your shaft, load and workflow. Common lifting configurations include 1:1 and 2:1. The complete setup depends on the shaft, load and installation method.

Common configuration

1:1 lifting configuration

The hoist travels with the platform. One part of rope supports the load.

Common configuration

2:1 lifting configuration

The hoist stays fixed. The rope runs through a pulley on the platform, so two parts of rope support the load.

In the shaft

Lift people and equipment where the work happens.

During elevator installation the traction hoist lifts personnel and equipment in the shaft. The wire rope passes through the hoist instead of winding onto a drum, so lifting height follows the length of rope deployed.

The hoist is selected after the job: the shaft, the load, and the power on site.

One setup

Configured around the installation

The hoist is one part of the setup. These are the variables we use to define it.

  • LoadPersonnel and equipment to be lifted.
  • Power supply208 V, single-phase or three-phase, 60 Hz, matched to the model.
  • Lifting configurationHow your team installs, including common 1:1 and 2:1 setups.
  • Wire ropeFAS-authorized 3/8 in (9.5 mm) steel wire rope.
  • ControlsControl box and connector for the selected phase.
  • AccessoriesMounting and jobsite items confirmed with the quote.

Traction

Why a traction hoist

  • No drumThe rope passes through the hoist. It is not stored on a drum.
  • Height follows the ropeLifting height is set by the wire rope deployed.
  • Personnel and equipmentThe hoist is used to lift crews and equipment inside the shaft during installation.
  • US power208 V / 60 Hz. Single-phase where three-phase power is not available. Three-phase for the 1,500 lb and 2,200 lb models.
  • Secondary brakeAn independent OSLA device locks the wire rope if descent speed exceeds the rated limit.
  • Installation setupModel, rope, controls and accessories are confirmed together for the shaft.
US and Canada

Certifications on the US range

North American UNILIFT models are certified to UL 1323 and CSA Z271 for powered suspended scaffold and suspended access applications. FAS-Tech also configures these traction hoists for elevator installation.

Certification applies to the specified hoist model and configuration. Those listings do not, by themselves, certify the elevator installation.
