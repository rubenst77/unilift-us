The system

How the system works

The UNILIFT is the traction hoist. The primary rope passes through it, and an independent safety rope runs to the OSLA.

Components

System components

Six parts. The hoist is ours. Platform, rigging and the rest of the access equipment are defined for each installation.

01

Traction hoists

UNILIFT moves along the primary wire rope.

02

Working platform

The deck the hoist travels with.

03

Primary wire ropes

FAS-authorized 3/8 in (9.5 mm) rope. Height follows the rope deployed.

04

Independent safety system

OSLA locks the safety rope if descent exceeds the permitted speed.

05

Controls

Up, down and stop, in the IP55 control box.

06

Suspension and rigging

Roof support and rigging are defined separately for each installation.

Safety

Built around controlled lifting and independent safety

Traction drive

The rope passes through the hoist. It is not stored on a drum.

Independent OSLA safety system

Locks the safety rope if descent exceeds the permitted speed.

Upper limit protection

Upward travel stops at the top of the run.

Overload protection

Trips at 125% of the working load limit. The hoist stops rising and will only descend.
