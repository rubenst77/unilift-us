The application

The hoist, the rope, and the platform

On suspended access, the UNILIFT is the traction hoist in the system. The wire rope passes through the hoist instead of winding onto a drum, so working height follows the rope deployed.

This page focuses on the North American UNILIFT traction hoist and its role within suspended access systems. Platform, rigging and project-specific access equipment are defined separately for each installation.

How it works

The parts of the system

UNILIFT moves along the wire rope. The platform, rigging and other access equipment are defined separately for each installation.

Key benefits

The traction hoist

No drum

The rope passes through the hoist. It is not stored on a drum.

Height follows the rope

Lifting height is set by the rope deployed.

US power options

208 V / 60 Hz. Single-phase where three-phase power is not available.

Secondary safety

An independent OSLA device locks the rope if descent exceeds the rated limit.
