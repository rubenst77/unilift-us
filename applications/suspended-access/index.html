The application

The hoist, the rope, and the platform

On suspended access, the UNILIFT is the traction hoist in the system. The wire rope passes through the hoist instead of winding onto a drum, so working height follows the rope deployed. The platform, rigging and fall protection are part of the complete installation and are the responsibility of the employer and the qualified person on the job.

This page covers the US traction hoist. It does not describe a complete suspended platform, roof system, monorail or davit as a US-certified package.

  • Traction hoistUNILIFT, mounted in the suspended system.
  • Wire ropeFAS-authorized 3/8 in (9.5 mm) rope. Diameter alone is not enough.
  • Suspended platformThe working deck the hoist travels with or lifts.
  • Building accessExterior work such as the facade access already described on the US site.
